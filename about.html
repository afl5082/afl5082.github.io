Our Origin Story

It started in 2020 with a moment of frustration at a wedding buffet. Our founder, Adam LaCaria—a Data Engineer living with IBS—realized the "safe" option was a plain, unseasoned chicken breast while everyone else enjoyed a gourmet meal.

He saw a disconnect: The data on Low-FODMAP ingredients existed, but it wasn't being applied in professional kitchens. Catering was still operating on "gut feeling" rather than gut science.

Datatribute was born to bridge this gap. Operating out of the tech hubs of Northern Virginia and Huntsville, Alabama, we apply rigorous data analysis to ingredient sourcing and menu design. We prove that digestive health doesn't have to mean compromising on flavor.

The Science Behind The Food

In 2005, researchers at Monash University coined the term "FODMAP" (Fermentable Carbohydrates). For millions of people, these specific carbohydrates trigger pain and bloating.

Traditional catering often relies heavily on high-FODMAP triggers like garlic, onions, and wheat to build flavor. We take a different approach. We use molecular alternatives—garlic-infused oils (where the flavor is captured but the fermentable sugars are removed), aged cheeses, and alternative grains.

By leveraging Monash University data sets, we engineer menus that are scientifically safe for the sensitive gut, yet indistinguishable from standard fine dining for your other guests.
