Catering FAQ

Common questions about our data-driven food service

How much notice do you need for an order?

Because we use fresh, locally sourced ingredients to minimize waste, we prefer 48 hours notice for corporate lunches and 2 weeks for private parties. For weddings, we recommend booking 3-6 months in advance.

Is your food purely Low-FODMAP?

Yes. Every item on our menu is designed to be Low-FODMAP compliant based on Monash University standards. However, our culinary team ensures the food is seasoned and prepared so that guests without restrictions will enjoy it just as much.

Can you accommodate other allergies?

Absolutely. Our kitchens are naturally 95% Gluten-Free and can easily accommodate Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, and Vegan requests. Please list specific allergies in the contact form.

Do you offer tastings?

Yes, for weddings and large corporate contracts, we offer a "Data & Dining" tasting session where we review your guest demographics and let you sample the proposed menu.

Where do you deliver?

We actively serve the Northern Virginia area (Arlington, Alexandria, Fairfax) and Northern Alabama (Huntsville, Madison, Florence, The Shoals). Delivery fees may apply based on distance.
